**Name** : Waselni: send parcels abroad

**Short description** : Send parcels abroad with verified travellers and carriers going your way.

**Full description** :
Sending a parcel to family back home is expensive, and finding someone you can trust isn't easy. Waselni connects senders with travellers and carriers, individuals or professionals, who are already making the same trip.

📦 SENDERS
• Search trips by departure country, arrival country and transport mode (road or plane)
• Compare dates, available space, price per kg and reviews
• Post your shipping request with photos: carriers send you offers
• Accept the offer that suits you and track the shipment step by step

🚚 ✈️ TRAVELLERS AND CARRIERS
• Earn money from the free space in your luggage or vehicle
• Post your trips in seconds
• Find shipping requests on your route and make offers

💬 BUILT-IN MESSAGING
Chat directly with members, send photos or documents.

🛡️ YOUR GUARANTEES
• Every carrier gets their identity verified (ID document and proof of address)
• Professionals are checked (SIRET or Kbis extract) and show the "Pro" badge
• Reviews are only possible after a real delivery
• Two-factor authentication (2FA) and confidential documents
• One-tap reporting and a moderation team

🌍 In French, English and Arabic, with dark mode.

Waselni does not carry parcels itself: the platform connects members. Items banned by law or by transport companies are refused.
